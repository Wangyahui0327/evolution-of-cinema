Method: linked filters run on the unique film-level dataset using Crossfilter2. D3.js draws the custom charts. The original movie / genre / cast / crew / award tables are processed in advance; not all rows are duplicated in the published site.

Data caveats: Film years and countries describe entries in the source movie metadata rather than global production output. Production country does not represent viewer location or local box-office markets. Rotten Tomatoes data are missing for many titles. Award entries are not exhaustive and different awards have different eligibility rules; never treat missing award records as a confirmed loss. Genre labels reflect the source taxonomy (including some specialist categories); films may have more than one genre. Runtime, colour and aspect-ratio trends reflect only films with recorded technical metadata; missingness may vary by decade. No causal inference is claimed. Source: user-provided five-table movie metadata dataset.
